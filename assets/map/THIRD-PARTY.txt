location descriptions in extra/, and NotoSans-Medium glyph ranges.
The three external resource URLs in style.json were changed to relative
local paths. time-slices.json contains the project's generated timeMeta.ts
data as JSON. snapshot.json records the snapshot version and file size.
descriptions-index.json maps the snapshot's place headings to extra/ feature
IDs, preferring a record with a descriptive paragraph over a heading alone.
